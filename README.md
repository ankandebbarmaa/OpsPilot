# OpsPilot

**Your AI operations manager for small businesses.**
It watches your money, tells you what needs attention today, and takes action for you.

---

## What is OpsPilot?

OpsPilot is an AI agent built for small business owners who don't have time to chase invoices, track expenses, or watch their cash flow every day.

Every day, it looks at your business data and gives you one simple briefing:

- Which invoices are overdue, with a ready-to-send reminder
- Which expenses look unusual and might need a second look
- How many days of cash you have left at your current spending rate

It doesn't just report problems. It can also take action — like sending a payment reminder for you.

Think of it as an operations manager who never sleeps, never forgets a follow-up, and never misses a warning sign.

## Why we built it

Small business owners are busy running their business, not doing bookkeeping. Because of that:

- Unpaid invoices often sit unpaid far longer than they should
- Unusual or wasteful expenses go unnoticed
- Cash flow problems are usually spotted too late, not in advance

Nobody has time to check this manually every single day — so most owners simply don't. OpsPilot does it for them, automatically.

## How it works

1. **Collect** — OpsPilot pulls in a business's invoices and expenses
2. **Understand** — an AI agent reviews the data and finds what matters: overdue payments, unusual spending, cash runway risk
3. **Brief** — the owner opens their dashboard and sees one clear, plain-English summary of what needs attention today
4. **Act** — for things that can be handled automatically (like a payment reminder), OpsPilot drafts the message and can send it directly, with one click or fully automatically

## Core Features

- [ ] Overdue invoice detection with auto-drafted, ready-to-send reminder emails
- [ ] Expense anomaly detection with plain-language explanations
- [ ] Cash runway forecast ("you have ~11 days of cash left at this rate")
- [ ] One simple daily "Ops Brief" view — no digging through dashboards
